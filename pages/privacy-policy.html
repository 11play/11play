Policy Overview

How this Privacy Policy applies

This policy applies to the 11Play website, Google Sign-In, profile services, user-provided mobile information, Invite Your Friend sharing, the 500 BDT promotional Offer, tawk.to-powered Live Chat support and related technical information.

11
Platform scope

About 11Play

11Play is a Smart Web Access and promotional platform that provides structured access to independent third-party websites together with Google-connected profile, sharing, promotional Offer and support features.

11Play does not own, host or control the independent third-party gaming websites accessible through the platform.

11Play does not accept gaming deposits, process third-party gaming payments, control gaming outcomes or manage deposits and withdrawals belonging to third-party gaming websites.

The current 11Play 500 BDT Offer is a separately administered promotional benefit and does not represent a gaming balance, deposit account or withdrawal wallet operated for an independent third-party website.

Information Collection

Information we may use

The information used depends on the 11Play features selected by the user.

◎
Google account

Google Sign-In Information

When a user chooses Google Sign-In, 11Play may receive limited account information made available through Google and Firebase Authentication.

Profile name Used to identify and display the user's 11Play account.
Email address Used for authentication, account identity and security.
Profile photo Used as the account avatar where Google provides it.
Authentication identifier Used to maintain the user's profile and associate protected account information with the authenticated Firebase account.

11Play does not receive or store the user's Google password. Google and Firebase Authentication manage the Google authentication process.

#
User-provided information

Mobile and Profile Information

A signed-in user may provide a valid Bangladesh mobile number as part of the 11Play profile system.

Once a valid mobile number has been successfully saved to a profile, the current account system locks that number against ordinary user replacement or removal.

The current system does not require one mobile number to be globally unique across all 11Play profiles. The same mobile number may therefore appear on more than one account where the database rules permit it.

11Play does not currently use the profile mobile-number field as proof of physical SIM ownership and does not represent the stored number as independently OTP-verified by 11Play.

Profile information may also include registration date, last login information, account status and technical timestamps required for account administration and security.

11Play does not request debit-card numbers, credit-card numbers, online-banking passwords, mobile-wallet PINs or third-party gaming account passwords through the profile system.

↗
Sharing information

Invite Your Friend

11Play provides an Invite Your Friend sharing feature that allows users to share the official 11Play website and promotional information with other people.

  • Sharing uses the official 11Play website address.
  • The current Invite feature does not assign a personal referral code to the user.
  • The current Invite feature does not create a tracked referral relationship between two accounts.
  • The current Invite feature does not create referral reward balances or referral-payment entitlement.
  • The platform may provide promotional text or an Offer image as part of the normal sharing experience.
  • The device or application used for sharing may process the shared content according to that service's own privacy practices.
◷
Account information

Registration and Login Records

11Play may maintain limited timestamps and account state needed to operate and administer the user's authenticated profile.

  • Profile registration date and creation timestamp.
  • Last-login and related account-update timestamps.
  • Current account status such as active, suspended or blocked.
  • Google-connected account indicators required by the current profile data contract.
  • Schema and technical information required to keep profile records consistent with the current application version.

These records are used for normal account operation, administration, troubleshooting and security.

11Play does not use the current account model to measure a seven-day or hourly activity requirement for referral reward qualification.

The current website also does not use a permanent browser Web Device identifier as a requirement for obtaining the current 500 BDT promotional Offer.

Registration and login timestamps are account-operation records and should not be interpreted as proof that a user remained continuously active on the website.

◈
Promotional records

500 BDT Offer Administration

11Play may maintain limited administrative information relating to the current 500 BDT new-user promotional Offer.

When the authorized 11Play administrator confirms that an eligible user's Offer has actually been provided, an administrative Offer Paid record may be created for that user.

Offer-administration information may include whether the Offer has been marked paid, the time of the administrative action and the identity of the authorized administrator who recorded the action.

Offer Paid information is maintained separately from the ordinary user profile and is not intended to be readable or editable by normal users.

The current 11Play system does not maintain a user-facing promotional wallet, withdrawal balance or financial ledger for this Offer.

A recorded Offer Paid state is maintained for administrative consistency and is not intended to be casually reversed or deleted through normal user operations.

Purpose of Use

Why information is used

11Play uses account, support and promotional information only for defined platform, security, support and administration purposes.

✓
Permitted purposes

How We Use Information

  • Create, identify and maintain a secure 11Play profile.
  • Authenticate eligible users through verified Google Sign-In.
  • Display the user's Google-provided profile identity where appropriate.
  • Associate the authenticated Google email with the correct Firebase profile.
  • Store a user-provided Bangladesh mobile number as part of the profile where the user chooses to add it.
  • Enforce the current one-time mobile lock after successful profile registration of that number.
  • Maintain registration, last-login and account-status information required for normal account administration.
  • Provide Invite Your Friend sharing without creating a referral-tracking or referral-reward system.
  • Provide information and support relating to the current 500 BDT new-user promotional Offer.
  • Allow the authorized administrator to record whether the current Offer has already been provided.
  • Reduce duplicate promotional handling and investigate suspected misuse where reasonably necessary.
  • Provide Live Chat support through the third-party tawk.to chat service when the user chooses to start a chat.
  • Review and respond to support messages voluntarily submitted by users through Live Chat.
  • Protect platform integrity, security and reliability.

Promotional Abuse Prevention

Limited verification protects legitimate users

Authenticated account identity, profile information, account status, user-provided mobile information and administrative Offer records may be reviewed where reasonably necessary to detect duplicate promotional handling, unauthorized account activity or other misuse.

Information voluntarily provided during a Live Chat conversation may also be reviewed where reasonably necessary to answer a support request, verify the relevant Offer process or investigate suspected misuse.

These checks are used to protect the platform and the current promotional Offer. The current system does not use referral-code tracking, activity qualification, a user-facing reward wallet or a Web Device qualification system.

Technical Information

Device, usage and performance data

◫
Service operation

Technical and Usage Information

11Play and the infrastructure services used by the website may automatically process limited technical information needed to operate, secure, analyse and improve the platform.

  • Browser type, operating system and general device category.
  • Language, screen size and general application settings.
  • Pages, features and navigation events used within 11Play.
  • Authentication-session and request information needed by Firebase services.
  • Registration, login and application timestamps required by supported account features.
  • Local favourites or browsing-history information stored on the user's device where applicable.
  • Error, performance and diagnostic information.
  • Network or request information ordinarily processed by hosting, authentication, database, analytics, security or Live Chat infrastructure.

When the user chooses to start 11Play Live Chat, the tawk.to chat widget is loaded from tawk.to infrastructure. tawk.to may process chat messages submitted by the user together with technical information ordinarily required to deliver and secure the chat service, according to tawk.to's own privacy practices.

11Play does not automatically send the user's Google profile name, Gmail address, mobile number or Firebase profile record to tawk.to through the current Live Chat module. Information entered manually by the user into the chat may, however, be processed by the chat service.

The current 11Play web account system does not require a browser-generated Unique Web Device identifier for referral qualification or for the current 500 BDT promotional Offer.

11Play does not obtain an IMEI, Android serial number, hardware serial number, MAC address or another guaranteed immutable physical-device identifier through ordinary website access.

Browser storage may still be used for normal local application preferences, favourites, browsing history or other non-sensitive client-side application functions.

Clearing browser storage may therefore reset local preferences or locally stored application information, but it does not automatically delete protected profile records stored in Cloud Firestore.

Depending on the feature used, information may be processed through services including Google Analytics, Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, GitHub Pages and tawk.to Live Chat.

The current 11Play profile and Offer administration architecture uses GitHub Pages together with Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore for normal production account operations and does not require deployed Firebase Cloud Functions. Live Chat delivery is separately provided through tawk.to and is not stored in the 11Play Firestore chat database because no such internal chat database is used.

Browser Technology

Cookies and local device storage

◌
Local preferences

Cookies and Local Storage

11Play may use browser storage to maintain application preferences, favourites, browsing history, authentication-related state or other temporary application information needed by supported features.

The current Invite Your Friend feature shares the official 11Play website and does not require local referral attribution or a personal referral code.

Protected account identity, profile information and Offer administration records are maintained through Firebase services rather than being treated merely as local browser preferences.

Google Analytics, Firebase, tawk.to and linked third-party websites may use cookies, browser storage or similar technologies according to the services they provide and their applicable privacy practices.

The tawk.to widget is loaded when the user chooses to start Live Chat. Chat-related browser technologies may then be used by tawk.to to operate the conversation, maintain the chat session, deliver messages and support service security.

Users may clear cookies and local storage through their browser settings. Doing so may reset saved preferences, favourites, browsing history, Live Chat session information or other locally stored application state.

Clearing browser storage does not automatically remove protected Google-connected profile, email-association, mobile or Admin-only Offer records already stored in Cloud Firestore. It also does not necessarily delete information already submitted through a third-party Live Chat service.

Account Security Architecture

How protected account operations are controlled

◈
Firebase security

Authentication and Database Controls

11Play uses Firebase Authentication to establish authenticated Google account identity and Cloud Firestore to maintain protected profile, email-association and Offer-administration records.

Firestore Security Rules form an important security boundary for protected database access. Rules are designed to determine whether an authenticated account may read or write particular information and whether supported sensitive state changes are valid.

Supported multi-document operations may use Firestore transactions or batches together with Security Rules that validate related profile and email-association state before an operation is accepted by the database.

Ordinary users are not intended to have unrestricted access to administrative Offer records, another user's private profile or authority to mark their own promotional Offer as paid.

Live Chat is separate from this Firestore account-data architecture. The current Live Chat module loads the tawk.to service and does not create chat-message collections inside 11Play Cloud Firestore.

Information Security

How account information is protected

◈
Security controls

Data Security

11Play uses technical and administrative safeguards intended to reduce unauthorized access, alteration, misuse, accidental loss or improper disclosure of protected account information.

  • Verified Google authentication through Firebase Authentication for protected profile features.
  • User-specific access controls for private account records.
  • Immutable association between the authenticated Google email and its protected profile where required by the current data contract.
  • One-time locking of the user-provided mobile number after it has been successfully saved to the profile.
  • A fixed verified administrator identity for protected administrative functions.
  • Firestore Security Rules restricting unauthorized reads and writes.
  • Separation of ordinary user profile data from Admin-only Offer Paid information.
  • Restricted Offer Paid changes so normal users cannot create, reverse or delete administrative Offer records.
  • Deny-by-default protection for unsupported or removed account collections.
  • Separation of tawk.to Live Chat from protected 11Play Firestore account records.

No internet-based system or third-party communication service can guarantee absolute security. Users should protect their Google accounts, mobile devices and authenticated browser sessions from unauthorized access and should avoid sending confidential authentication information through Live Chat.

The current website does not represent browser-based access as a guaranteed physical-device fingerprint or permanent hardware identity.

✓
Administrative review

User and Offer Administration

Certain account and promotional operations may be reviewed by the authorized 11Play administrator.

The administrator may review registered-user information required by the current Admin Dashboard, including name, username, Gmail address, mobile number, registration date, last login, account status and Offer status.

11Play support may separately review information voluntarily submitted through tawk.to Live Chat for the purpose of providing support, supplying current Offer instructions, reviewing an Offer-related request or resolving a reported problem.

When the current 500 BDT promotional Offer has actually been provided to a user, the authorized administrator may mark that user's Offer status as paid.

This administrative action may create a protected Offer record containing the paid state, action time and administrator identity required to maintain consistent promotional records.

Administrative access does not authorize an ordinary user to read or modify protected Offer Paid information or another user's private profile.

Data Retention

How long information may be retained

◷
Retention period

Retention of Information

Google-connected profile, email-association, mobile, registration, login and administrative Offer records may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the account, provide support, maintain promotional consistency, investigate misuse, resolve disputes and meet applicable obligations.

Live Chat messages and related chat-service information are processed separately through tawk.to and may be retained by that service according to its applicable service configuration and privacy practices. 11Play does not maintain a duplicate internal Firestore chat-message database for the current tawk.to integration.

Some identity, mobile-lock or administrative Offer records may need to remain restricted from ordinary user modification or deletion so that account security and promotional records remain consistent.

Information that is no longer reasonably required may be deleted, anonymised or otherwise restricted where appropriate, subject to technical, security, record-integrity and applicable legal requirements.
