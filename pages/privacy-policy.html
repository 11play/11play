Policy Overview

How this Privacy Policy applies

This policy applies to the 11Play website, Google Sign-In, account services, referral programme, Unique Web Device Binding, activity-based referral qualification, promotional rewards, wallet records and supported reward-withdrawal features.

11
Platform scope

About 11Play

11Play is a Smart Web Access and promotional platform that provides structured access to independent third-party websites together with account, referral, reward and platform-update features.

11Play does not own, host or control the independent third-party gaming websites accessible through the platform.

11Play does not accept gaming deposits, process third-party gaming payments, control gaming outcomes or manage deposits and withdrawals belonging to third-party gaming websites.

Withdrawals handled within the 11Play account system relate only to eligible promotional referral rewards provided directly through the 11Play promotional programme.

User-provided information

Mobile and Withdrawal Information

A user may provide a valid Bangladesh mobile number for referral qualification and account-related promotional features.

A mobile number accepted for referral qualification is reserved for one 11Play account. Once accepted and locked by the profile system, the mobile number cannot ordinarily be replaced by the user. This supports referral integrity and promotional-abuse prevention.

11Play uses mobile-number uniqueness as an account and anti-abuse condition. The website does not use SMS OTP to establish ownership of the SIM or mobile-wallet account.

When requesting withdrawal of an eligible 11Play promotional reward, the user may provide a supported mobile-wallet method and wallet number for bKash, Nagad or Rocket.

This information may be used to validate the withdrawal request, maintain financial records and allow the authorized administrator to process an approved promotional reward withdrawal.

11Play does not request debit-card numbers, credit-card numbers, online-banking passwords, mobile-wallet PINs or third-party gaming account passwords.

Referral information

Referral and Reward Records

11Play records referral information required to connect an invited user with the correct referrer and determine whether the published promotional reward conditions have been met.

  • Unique referral code and referral link.
  • Referrer and referred-account attribution.
  • Referral capture, pending, qualified, rejected and rewarded status, together with historical referral-status records where applicable.
  • Verified Google-account qualification state.

  • Eligible Active Day progress required by the promotional programme.
  • Reward review, approval, rejection and credit records.
  • Wallet transactions and supported promotional reward withdrawal records.
◷
Activity information

Eligible Active Days and Activity Records

Referral qualification requires activity on seven different Bangladesh calendar dates. Each qualifying date requires at least two hours of eligible active use before that date can count as one Eligible Active Day.

  • Number of completed Eligible Active Days.
  • Accepted activity seconds for the current Bangladesh calendar date.
  • Current-day completion state and required daily activity threshold.
  • Server-recorded activity checkpoint and last-active timestamps.
  • Activity-policy version and referral-qualification completion state.

At most one Eligible Active Day can be credited for a Bangladesh calendar date. Extra activity on a completed date does not create additional Eligible Active Days, and many hours accumulated on one date cannot replace activity required on other dates.

Firestore server time is used as the authoritative time source for accepted activity checkpoints. Browser clock changes do not determine referral eligibility.

The browser may apply visibility, focus, online-state and recent-interaction checks before attempting an activity checkpoint. Server-side timing and database rules remain the authority for whether activity is accepted.

Browser-based activity controls can reduce simple automated or overlapping activity abuse, but a normal website cannot technically prove continuous human attention at every moment.

◈
Financial records

Wallet and Promotional Reward Records

11Play may maintain an internal promotional-reward wallet for eligible referral rewards.

Wallet information may include available balance, held balance, total promotional rewards earned, completed withdrawals and immutable transaction records required to maintain an accurate account history.

When a withdrawal request is submitted, the requested amount may move from available balance to held balance while the request is waiting for administrative review.

A submitted withdrawal cannot be edited, deleted or cancelled by the ordinary user. The authorized administrator may approve the pending request or reject it. Approval finalizes the withdrawal accounting, while rejection returns the held amount to available balance.

Historical withdrawal records may contain older payment, cancellation or reference fields. Those historical fields do not create a current user-cancellation right and may remain preserved for record integrity.

These wallet records relate to 11Play promotional rewards and are not a wallet for deposits, gaming balances or funds held with an independent third-party gaming website.

Referral verification protects legitimate users

These checks are used to protect the promotional programme, determine reward eligibility, prevent duplicate reward credit and investigate suspected misuse.

Technical Information

Device, usage and performance data

◫
Service operation

Technical and Usage Information

11Play and the infrastructure services used by the website may automatically process limited technical information needed to operate, secure, analyse and improve the platform.

  • Browser type, operating system and general device category.
  • Language, screen size and general application settings.
  • Pages, features and navigation events used within 11Play.
  • Application visibility, focus, online state and recent interaction state used by browser-side activity controls.
  • Activity checkpoint, last-active and related application state required for supported referral features.
  • A randomly generated Unique Web Device identifier used as part of the referral anti-abuse system.
  • Error, performance and diagnostic information.
  • Network or request information ordinarily processed by hosting, authentication, database, analytics or security infrastructure.

The Unique Web Device identifier is generated by the browser using random data and may be stored in local browser storage and associated with the authenticated account through protected Firestore records.

This identifier is a browser-based anti-abuse binding. 11Play does not obtain an IMEI, Android serial number, hardware serial number or another guaranteed immutable physical-device identifier through the website.

Clearing browser storage, using another browser profile, private or incognito browsing, or moving to another browser environment may create a different browser identifier. If an account is already bound to another identifier, this can prevent referral qualification or eligible activity from being accepted on the new browser environment.

A web-device binding conflict does not by itself mean that the user owns a different physical device. It means that the browser identifier presented to 11Play does not match the identifier already associated with the account or is already reserved by another account.

Depending on the feature used, information may be processed through services including Google Analytics, Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore and GitHub Pages.

The current 11Play profile, referral, activity, wallet and withdrawal architecture uses GitHub Pages together with Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore for normal production account operations and does not require deployed Firebase Cloud Functions.

Browser Technology

Cookies and local device storage

◌
Local preferences

Cookies and Local Storage

11Play may use browser storage to maintain application preferences, favourites, browsing history, referral attribution, the Unique Web Device identifier, authentication-related state or other temporary application information needed by supported features.

Referral attribution may temporarily be stored in the browser when a user arrives through a referral link so that the referral can be validated after eligible Google Sign-In. Browser-stored attribution does not itself approve a referral or create a reward.

The browser-generated Unique Web Device identifier may also be retained in local storage. The corresponding account binding, where successfully established, is maintained through protected Firestore records and is not merely a browser preference.

Google Analytics, Firebase and linked third-party websites may use cookies or similar technologies according to the services they provide and their applicable privacy practices.

Users may clear cookies and local storage through their browser settings. Doing so may reset saved preferences, pending referral attribution or the locally stored web-device identifier.

Clearing browser storage does not automatically remove protected account, mobile, referral, device-binding, activity, wallet, withdrawal or audit records already stored in Cloud Firestore. A newly generated browser identifier may also fail to match an existing account binding.

Account Security Architecture

How protected account operations are controlled

◈
Firebase security

Authentication and Database Controls

11Play uses Firebase Authentication to establish authenticated account identity and Cloud Firestore to maintain protected profile, mobile reservation, web-device binding, referral, activity, wallet, withdrawal and related records.

Firestore Security Rules form an important security boundary for protected database access. Rules are designed to determine whether an authenticated account may read or write particular information and whether supported sensitive state changes are valid.

Sensitive multi-document operations may use Firestore transactions together with Security Rules that validate related account, mobile reservation, web-device, referral, activity, wallet, transaction, reward or audit state before an operation is accepted by the database.

Ordinary users are not intended to have unrestricted access to administrative records or unrestricted authority to create promotional reward balances, approve referrals, alter another account's device binding or process another user's withdrawal.

Information Security

How account information is protected

◈
Security controls

Data Security

11Play uses technical and administrative safeguards intended to reduce unauthorized access, alteration, misuse, accidental loss or improper disclosure of protected account information.

  • Verified Google authentication through Firebase Authentication for protected profile features.
  • User-specific access controls for private account records.
  • Global reservation of qualifying Bangladesh mobile numbers to reduce duplicate promotional identities.
  • Unique Web Device Binding used as an additional browser- based referral anti-abuse layer.
  • A fixed verified administrator identity for protected administrative functions.
  • Firestore Security Rules restricting unauthorized reads and writes.
  • Transaction and state validation for supported referral, activity, wallet, reward and withdrawal operations.
  • Immutable or restricted financial transaction and audit records where required by the application architecture.
  • Validation designed to prevent self-referrals, duplicate reward credit, device-binding abuse and unauthorized wallet changes.

No internet-based system can guarantee absolute security. Users should protect their Google accounts, mobile devices and authenticated browser sessions from unauthorized access.

Browser-based device binding is an anti-abuse control and is not represented as a guaranteed physical-device fingerprint or permanent hardware identifier.

✓
Administrative review

Referral and Withdrawal Review

Certain promotional operations require review by the authorized 11Play administrator, including qualified referral review, promotional reward approval or rejection and pending withdrawal review.

A qualified referral does not automatically create a wallet reward. The authorized administrator may approve the qualified referral, resulting in the applicable promotional reward being credited once, or reject the referral without crediting the reward.

A submitted withdrawal remains pending until the authorized administrator approves or rejects it. Ordinary users cannot cancel, edit or delete a submitted withdrawal request.

Administrative actions may create or update referral status, wallet transaction, reward-event and audit records needed to document the decision and maintain account consistency.

Administrative access does not authorize an ordinary user to modify protected financial or administrative information.
