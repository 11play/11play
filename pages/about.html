About 11Play

11Play — Smart Web Access A simple platform for organized access to independent third-party gaming sites, Live Chat support, optional Google profiles and promotional offers.

11
Platform Overview

What is 11Play?

11Play is a Smart Web Access platform created for users who access multiple third-party gaming sites and want a simpler, organized way to find and access those services from one place.

The platform also provides Live Chat support, optional Google-connected profile features and current promotional information.

✓
Services

What 11Play Provides

  • Organized access to independent third-party gaming sites.
  • Live Chat support for access and platform-related help.
  • Optional Google-connected profile features.
  • Current promotional information and new-user offers.
—
Platform Scope

What 11Play Does Not Do

  • 11Play does not own or operate third-party gaming sites.
  • 11Play does not accept deposits or withdrawals.
  • 11Play does not process banking, cash or third-party gaming transactions.
  • External transactions remain between the user and the respective third-party service.
✦
User Support

Live Chat and Optional Profile

Creating a Google-connected 11Play profile is optional. When a user chooses to create one, limited information needed for the profile may be processed through Google and Firebase.

11Play also provides Live Chat support for users who need help with platform access or current offers.

!
Important

Independent Third-Party Sites

Gaming sites accessed through 11Play are independent third-party services. Their accounts, rules, transactions, promotions and availability are controlled by their respective operators.
