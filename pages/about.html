About the platform

What is 11Play?

11Play is a browser-based Smart Web Access and promotional platform designed to provide users with a fast, unified and structured way to discover and access independent third-party gaming websites and online services.

The platform also provides Google-connected account features, service updates, Invite Your Friend sharing, promotional Offer information and Live Chat support.

The current Live Chat service is delivered through tawk.to. The tawk.to widget is loaded when the user chooses to start Live Chat so that the user can communicate directly with 11Play support.

Scope and Responsibility

What 11Play does—and what it does not do

The platform separates its own access, account, support and promotional services from the independent websites users may access through 11Play.

New User Promotion

500 BDT new-user promotional Offer

Eligible new users may receive a 500 BDT promotional Offer by following the current 11Play Offer process. Users should first contact 11Play through Live Chat and obtain the relevant target-site link and instructions before registering.

The current Live Chat service is powered by tawk.to. Users can open the 11Play Live Chat page and select Start Live Chat to begin the support conversation.

The user must then register through the supplied link, complete the applicable requirements communicated for the target service and return to 11Play Live Chat to request the Offer.

The Offer is handled manually and remains subject to the published conditions and verification process. It is a promotional benefit provided through the 11Play Offer process and is not a deposit, gaming winning or transaction processed on behalf of a third-party website.

Data practices

Privacy & Data

11Play is designed to use limited account information for the operation of Google-connected profiles, account security, support and the current promotional Offer process.

Google account information Profile name, verified email address and profile photo where available through Google sign-in.
User-provided mobile number Stored with the user's profile when the user chooses to add it and locked after successful registration.
Account timestamps Registration and last-login information used for normal account operation and administration.
Offer administration Limited Admin-only information may be maintained to record whether the 500 BDT Offer has already been provided.

This information is used for account operation, authentication, security, support, administration and prevention of duplicate promotional handling where applicable.

Access to protected account information is restricted through Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore Security Rules. Normal users can access only the account information permitted for their own authenticated profile.

Live Chat is handled separately through tawk.to. Information voluntarily entered into a Live Chat conversation may be processed through tawk.to so that 11Play support can receive and respond to the message.

The current Live Chat integration does not automatically send the user's protected Google-connected Firestore profile, saved mobile number or other private account record to tawk.to.

Users should never send passwords, PINs, OTPs, recovery codes or other private authentication information through Live Chat.

Detailed information is available in the 11Play Privacy Policy .
