About the platform

What is 11Play?

11Play is a browser-based Smart Web Access and promotional platform designed to provide users with a fast, unified and structured way to discover and access independent third-party gaming websites and online services.

The platform also provides account features, service updates, referral tracking, reward information and supported withdrawal services for eligible promotional rewards.

Scope and Responsibility

What 11Play does—and what it does not do

The platform separates its own promotional services from the independent websites users may access through 11Play.

✓
Platform services

What We Do

  • Provide a unified browsing and navigation experience.
  • Organize independent third-party websites in a structured, mobile-friendly interface.
  • Provide platform notices, updates and account services.
  • Operate a promotional referral program for eligible users who invite friends through permanent referral links.
  • Provide cash-withdrawable promotional rewards after all applicable referral conditions are completed and verified.
—
Platform limitations

What We Do Not Do

  • We do not own, host or control third-party gaming websites.
  • We do not accept deposits or process payments, withdrawals or financial transactions for third-party websites.
  • We do not operate third-party betting accounts, place wagers on behalf of users or control gaming outcomes.
  • We do not guarantee third-party content, offers, availability, transactions or payment performance.
Promotional Referral Program

Invite friends and earn verified rewards

Users may invite friends through their permanent referral links. A promotional reward becomes eligible only after the referred user completes all published conditions and the referral passes the required verification checks.

Approved promotional rewards may be requested through supported withdrawal methods such as Nagad, bKash or Rocket, subject to account verification, availability and the applicable withdrawal rules.

These rewards are provided directly by 11Play as part of its own promotional activities. They are not deposits, gaming winnings or transaction proceeds from any third-party website.
