Protected account operations

Authentication and Firestore Security

Protected 11Play profile features use Firebase Authentication to identify verified Google users and Cloud Firestore to store account, referral, activity, wallet, withdrawal and related records.

Firestore Security Rules restrict unauthorized database access and validate supported state changes. Sensitive multi-document operations may use authenticated Firestore transactions so related profile, mobile, device, referral, activity, wallet, reward, withdrawal and audit records can be checked and updated consistently.

Unique Web Device Binding is used as a browser-based Device Anti-Abuse Layer for applicable referral and activity checks. It does not represent an IMEI, Android serial number or guaranteed immutable physical-device identifier.

The current production profile, referral, activity, wallet and withdrawal architecture does not depend on deployed Firebase Cloud Functions for normal account operations.

Support Knowledge Base

Frequently Asked Questions

01 What is 11Play?

11Play is a browser-based Smart Web Access and promotional platform. It organizes access to independent third-party websites and provides verified Google account, referral, activity, promotional reward, wallet and platform-update features.

External websites remain independently operated. 11Play does not control their accounts, transactions, offers, content or outcomes.

02 Does 11Play operate the external gaming websites?

No. Third-party websites accessible through 11Play are owned and managed by their respective operators.

11Play does not manage their user accounts, deposits, gaming activity, payment decisions, gaming outcomes or withdrawals. Users must review the terms and support channels of each external provider.

03 Does 11Play process third-party deposits or gaming withdrawals?

No. 11Play does not accept gaming deposits or process payments and withdrawals for third-party gaming websites.

A withdrawal processed through the 11Play account system applies only to eligible promotional referral rewards provided by 11Play. It is not a gaming-site deposit, winning balance or gaming withdrawal.

04 What is the 11Play referral programme?

Verified signed-in users receive a unique referral code and referral link that may be shared with friends. Guest sharing uses only the main 11Play website URL and does not attribute a referral reward to a guest.

A referred user must be correctly attributed and complete the verified Google account, unique Bangladesh mobile number, Unique Web Device Binding and Eligible Active Day requirements.

Once an authoritative referral relationship has been established, the referrer cannot be changed by another browser referral link or locally stored referral code.

05 Does sharing my referral link automatically earn the reward?

No. Sharing a referral link or creating an account does not automatically create a payable promotional reward.

The referred user must have a verified Google account, a globally unique valid Bangladesh mobile number, a valid Unique Web Device Binding and seven completed Eligible Active Days.

After those requirements are complete, the referral becomes Qualified and waits for Admin review. Only an approved qualified referral credits the referrer with the one-time ৳1000 reward.

06 Why is my referral still pending?

A referral remains pending until every required qualification condition has been completed.

The current requirements are a verified Google account, a valid Bangladesh mobile number reserved uniquely to that account, a matching Unique Web Device Binding and seven different Eligible Active Days.

Each Eligible Active Day requires at least 7,200 seconds, or 120 minutes, of eligible activity on one Bangladesh calendar date. Completing many hours on one date cannot create multiple Eligible Active Days.

When all conditions are complete, the referral may move to Qualified status and become available for Admin review.

07 Why was my referral rejected?

A qualified referral may be rejected during Admin review when final eligibility validation does not pass or when a promotional rule has been violated.

Possible reasons include self-referral, invalid attribution, duplicate accounts, mobile-number conflicts, device-binding conflicts, scripted or abnormal activity, incomplete qualification or repeated reward attempts.

A rejected referral does not credit the ৳1000 referral reward.

08 Which promotional reward withdrawal methods are supported?

Supported promotional reward-withdrawal methods are bKash, Nagad and Rocket.

The minimum withdrawal amount is ৳1000, and every withdrawal amount must be a whole-number multiple of ৳1000.

A withdrawal may be submitted only against sufficient Available Balance in the user's 11Play promotional wallet. Users should carefully verify the selected provider and recipient number before submission.

09 What happens to my balance after I submit a withdrawal?

When an eligible withdrawal request is successfully submitted, the requested amount moves from Available Balance to Held Balance while the request remains pending.

A submitted withdrawal cannot be edited, deleted or cancelled by the ordinary user.

If Admin approves the request, the corresponding held amount is finalized as withdrawn and Total Withdrawn is increased.

If Admin rejects the request, the held amount is returned to Available Balance.

10 What should I do after entering the wrong withdrawal number?

Contact 11Play support immediately and include the connected Google account email, withdrawal request ID, approximate submission time and the issue with the submitted provider or wallet number.

A submitted withdrawal cannot be edited, cancelled or deleted by the user, so the provider and number should always be checked carefully before submission.

Never send a bKash, Nagad or Rocket PIN, OTP, password or security code. A completed external transfer may not be reversible.

11 What information does 11Play receive through Google Sign-In?

11Play may receive the Google profile name, email address, profile photo where available, sign-in provider information and a unique Firebase Authentication identifier.

This limited information is used for account identity, authentication, security and protected account features such as referrals, activity, rewards, wallet history and withdrawals.

12 Does 11Play receive or store my Google password?

No. Google and Firebase Authentication manage the Google Sign-In process. 11Play does not receive or store the user's Google password.

Never send a Google password, recovery code, OTP or authentication code to anyone claiming to provide 11Play support.

13 Why does 11Play require a Bangladesh mobile number?

A valid Bangladesh mobile number is required as part of referral qualification and promotional-abuse prevention.

The canonical stored format is +8801XXXXXXXXX. One normalized mobile number may be reserved by only one 11Play account.

Once a valid mobile number has been accepted and locked by the profile system, the user cannot ordinarily replace it later.

The uniqueness check prevents reuse of the same mobile number across accounts. It is not an SMS OTP check and does not prove ownership of the SIM.

14 How do 11Play Eligible Active Days work?

Referral qualification requires seven different Eligible Active Days on seven different Bangladesh calendar dates.

Each eligible date requires at least 7,200 seconds, or 120 minutes, of accepted activity. Only one Eligible Active Day can be credited for the same Bangladesh calendar date.

Activity is credited through server-time checkpoints. After the day's server anchor is established, a normal successful checkpoint is accepted only after the required interval. A valid 15-minute checkpoint adds 900 seconds.

If a checkpoint is attempted too early, it does not accelerate progress. If the gap becomes too long, the system may resume from a new checkpoint anchor without granting the missed interval.

Browser visibility, focus, online state and recent interaction are used as client-side activity gates, but protected Firestore state and server time remain the qualification authority.

15 What is Unique Web Device Binding?

Unique Web Device Binding is a Device Anti-Abuse Layer used with referral qualification and eligible activity.

The browser creates a cryptographically random local identifier and the protected account record binds that identifier to the authenticated user. A device identifier already reserved by another account cannot be reused for referral qualification.

This is browser-based protection. 11Play does not claim that a website can read an immutable physical hardware identifier such as an IMEI, Android serial number or guaranteed device serial.

Clearing browser site data, using another browser profile or using private browsing can create a different local identifier. If the new local identifier does not match the account's existing authoritative binding, referral qualification or eligible activity may be blocked.

Installing an APK or using a web wrapper is not a referral qualification requirement and provides no additional qualification advantage.

16 Does 11Play sell users' personal information?

No. 11Play does not sell personal information to advertisers.

Limited information may be processed through services needed for authentication, Cloud Firestore database operation, analytics, GitHub Pages hosting, security or delivery of an approved promotional reward.

17 How does 11Play protect account and wallet information?

Protected account features use verified Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore access controls and Firestore Security Rules intended to prevent unauthorized reads and writes.

Supported sensitive operations use authenticated Firestore transactions together with state validation for profile, mobile, device, referral, activity, wallet, reward, withdrawal, transaction and audit records.

Administrative decisions are restricted to the authorized verified administrator identity. Ordinary users cannot directly approve referral rewards, create promotional wallet credit, approve withdrawals or process another user's protected records.

No internet-based service can guarantee absolute security. Users must also protect their Google account, mobile device and authenticated browser session.

18 Can I request correction or deletion of my account information?

Users may contact 11Play regarding account-information correction or deletion requests. The request should identify the relevant account and clearly describe the information concerned.

A locked mobile number, established referral relationship or protected financial record cannot necessarily be changed merely through an ordinary client-side request.

Some referral, reward, wallet, transaction, withdrawal or administrative audit records may need to remain retained or restricted for security, transaction integrity, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, completed reward history or applicable obligations.

19 Why is the 11Play page or account feature not loading correctly?

Loading or account problems may be caused by unstable internet, outdated cached files, disabled JavaScript, blocked Google authentication, expired account state or a temporary hosting, Firebase or network-provider issue.

Refresh the page, verify the internet connection, switch between mobile data and Wi-Fi, update the browser and sign in again where appropriate.

Be careful before clearing all 11Play site data when your account already has a Unique Web Device Binding. Clearing browser storage removes the locally stored device identifier and a newly generated identifier may no longer match the account's authoritative binding.

If a device mismatch occurs, include the account email, browser name and information about whether site data, browser profiles or private browsing were recently changed when contacting support.

20 What should I include in a support request?

Include the issue category, connected Google account email where relevant, approximate date and time, device, operating system, browser and a clear description of what happened.

For referral problems, include the relevant referral code, current referral status and whether the problem concerns mobile uniqueness, device binding or Eligible Active Days.

For withdrawal problems, include the withdrawal request ID, amount, provider and approximate submission time. Do not send wallet PINs, OTPs or passwords.

Include a screenshot only where useful. Remove passwords, OTPs, PINs, recovery codes and unnecessary personal information before sending it.

Standard support requests normally aim for a response within 24–48 hours. Account-security, referral, promotional-abuse and withdrawal reviews may require additional time.
